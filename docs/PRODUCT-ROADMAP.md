# BVOS Elite Performance — Product Roadmap

## Product Promise
Give basketball players a clear answer to one question every day: **What can I do today to become a better basketball player?**

Elite Performance is a subscription-based digital development platform built around three pillars:

1. **Basketball Development** — drills, workouts, shooting, ball handling, finishing, footwork, and position development.
2. **Performance Training** — strength, conditioning, mobility, speed, agility, explosiveness, and recovery.
3. **The Chase** — mindset, leadership, discipline, confidence, and BVOS motivational content.

## v0.1 — Foundation
- WordPress plugin bootstrap
- Elite Performance member role
- Drills content type
- Programs content type
- The Chase content type
- Admin content-management shell
- Subscriber dashboard shortcode

## v0.2 — Membership
- Paid monthly and annual access architecture
- Complimentary membership support
- Subscriber access-control service
- Account and membership status
- Protected premium content
- Public previews for locked content

## v0.3 — Training Experience
- Drill taxonomy and filtering
- Program > week > workout structure
- Video embeds
- Sets, reps, duration, rest, equipment, level, and coaching cues
- Start/complete workout flow
- Mobile-first workout player

## v0.4 — Athlete Dashboard
- Today's Chase
- Continue Training
- This Week
- My Programs
- Favorites
- Completion history
- Progress indicators

## v0.5 — Performance Tracking
- Weight used per exercise
- Personal records
- Training history
- Streaks and achievements
- Performance testing

## Design Direction
The portal should inherit the BVOS Basketball visual system and feel native to the existing website: high-energy, premium, mobile-first, and built around the BVOS identity rather than looking like a generic WordPress membership plugin.
